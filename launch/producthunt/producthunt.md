## Primeiro comentário (do maker)

Hey Product Hunt! 👋

I built DeadNoise because I didn't want to pay a monthly subscription (or send my voice to someone's cloud) just to stop my keyboard and fan from showing up on calls.

macOS already ships an excellent on-device voice isolation model, but it only works inside some apps. DeadNoise puts it in front of every call app: it adds a virtual microphone, "DeadNoise Microphone", that only carries your voice. Pick it in Meet, Zoom, Teams or anything else and you're done.

While I was at it, I added two things I always wanted in the menu bar:
• Volume per app (turn the music down without turning the meeting down)
• Record & transcribe meetings, split into "Me" and "Others", all on your Mac (transcription needs macOS 26)

It's MIT-licensed, the installers are signed and notarized by Apple, and the app updates itself. It's in English and Portuguese.

Honest limits: it cleans *your* mic, not the other participants' audio, and it needs macOS 15+.

I'd love your feedback, especially if you try it with Bluetooth headsets. 🙏
