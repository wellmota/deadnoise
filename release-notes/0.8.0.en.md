- **Per-tab volume**: in Chrome (and Edge, Brave, Arc and Vivaldi), every tab playing sound shows up under the browser in the menu, with its own volume (0–100%) and mute. Two meetings in two tabs, two sliders. Click a tab's name to jump to it.
- Requires the DeadNoise extension, bundled with the app: in the menu, click the tabs icon next to Chrome and follow the two steps.
