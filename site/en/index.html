DeadNoise: microphone noise cancellation for Mac

Kill my noise

Flip the switch. This is what your call hears today: keyboard, fan, dog.

Noise cancellation for your Mac’s microphone. Kill the noise before it reaches the call. Everything runs locally.

Download for macOS

Free and open source. Installer signed and verified by Apple. Updates itself. macOS 15 or later. The app’s interface is currently in Brazilian Portuguese.

Three things.
Done right.

01

Kill my noise

A virtual microphone sends only your voice to Meet, Teams, Zoom or any other app. The noise dies before it leaves your Mac.

02

Per-app volume

Turn the music down without turning the meeting down. Every app gets its own slider, from 0 to 150%, right in the menu bar.

03

Record and transcribe

Records the call and writes a transcript split into “Me” and “Others”. The audio never leaves your computer.

How it
works

  1. DeadNoise listens to your real microphone.Only while an app is on a call.
  2. macOS voice isolation removes the noise.On-device processing, no cloud, no account.
  3. Your clean voice comes out of “DeadNoise Microphone”.That’s the microphone you pick in your calling app.
Download for macOS
  1. Open DeadNoise.dmg and drag the app to Applications.
  2. Open DeadNoise: it lives in the menu bar, with the skull icon.
  3. Turn on Cancelar meu ruído (“kill my noise”) and click Instalar microfone virtual (“install virtual microphone”). macOS asks for your password and audio restarts for a few seconds.

Prefer an installer that does it all? Get the .pkg.

Install from the Terminal 
# build and install from source
git clone https://github.com/wellmota/deadnoise
cd deadnoise
./build.sh install          # the app
./Driver/build.sh install   # the virtual microphone

Requires the Xcode command line tools. The second command asks for your password.

Signed and verified by Apple. macOS 15 or later. Transcription requires macOS 26. Recording calls may require the consent of other participants.

Frequently
asked

Is DeadNoise free?

Yes. It’s free software under the MIT license: no account, no subscription and no minute limits. The code is on GitHub.

Does it work with Google Meet, Zoom and Microsoft Teams?

Yes. DeadNoise adds a virtual microphone called “DeadNoise Microphone”. Any calling app that lets you pick a microphone can use it: select it in the app’s audio settings.

Is my audio sent to the cloud?

No. Noise cancellation uses macOS’s own voice isolation and runs entirely on your Mac. Meeting recordings and transcripts stay on your computer too. The app has no account and no server.

Which Macs does it run on?

macOS 15 or later. The app is universal (Apple Silicon and Intel). Meeting transcription requires macOS 26. The app’s interface is currently in Brazilian Portuguese.

Is it an alternative to Krisp?

For removing noise from your own microphone, yes: it’s free, open source and runs locally. DeadNoise doesn’t remove noise from other participants and has no cloud features.

Why does macOS ask for my password during installation?

To install the virtual microphone, which is a system audio driver. It is asked for at install time, and again only when an update ships a new virtual microphone. The app and the installers are signed and verified (notarized) by Apple.

How do I get updates?

The app checks for new versions once a day and lets you know in its menu. Under Options (…) you can check right away or let updates install on their own. Every update is signed, and the app rejects any update whose signature doesn’t match.
